# Privacy notice

*First draft, in plain English. To be reviewed before it is relied on.*

This explains what we keep about you and your jobs, and why.

## What we store
- **Your account:** your email address, your name if you give it, and your password (kept scrambled, so we cannot read it).
- **Your business:** registration number and the details we read from the Environment Agency public register, trading name, mobile number, base town, van registrations, insurer and expiry date (if you give them), and your logo.
- **Each job:** photos, the town or area and postcode, the job type, what was collected (items, quantity, waste description and code), the customer's name if you add it, which van, tip receipt details, and the video we make.
- **Your acceptances:** which version of these documents you accepted, and when.

## Why we keep it
- To make your videos and run the app.
- To give you transfer notes and a records export for your waste paperwork.
- To keep a record of each job for at least two years, as carriers are expected to.
- To check a registration, and to deal fairly with any report of fly-tipping.

## Videos are public once shared
When you share a video, anyone with the link can see it, and it may be posted where you choose. Photos and the job record are not public. The registered-carrier mark, your trading name and your logo appear on the video. Your insurance, mobile number and van registrations are not put on videos.

## Who sees it
- Only you (the owner) see your business details and job records. Crew see only what they need to take photos and make videos.
- We use trusted services to run the app (hosting, voice and writing). They process data for us and do not use it for their own purposes.
- We do not sell your data. We may use anonymised, combined information for research and reporting, as set out in the Terms.

## How long
- Job records: at least two years. If you delete a job's photos and video, the record stays.
- Account details: until you delete your account. Your jobs stay with the business record.

## Your rights under UK GDPR
You can ask to see, correct, or erase what we hold about you, to object to how we use it, or to take a copy. Some job records must be kept for two years and cannot be erased before then. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
