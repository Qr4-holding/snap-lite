*First draft, in plain English. To be reviewed before it is relied on.*

## Who can use it
- The app is for waste carriers registered in England (or the equivalent register elsewhere in the UK). You must give us your registration number and keep it current.
- A lower tier registration covers your own waste only. You can still make videos, but the registered-carrier mark is not shown on them.
- You are responsible for everyone you give a login to.

## What you promise
- Every load you collect goes to a site that is licensed or permitted to take it, and you keep the receipt.
- You record your jobs honestly. Photos and details must be of the real job.
- You do not use the app to claim anything about your business that is not true.

## Fly-tipping
- You never fly-tip, and you report fly-tipping you find.
- If fly-tipping by you or your business is proven, we end your place on Clean Up UK and CleanUpSnap. We may withdraw your registered-carrier mark and your listing while a report is looked into, and we will tell you why.

- You keep ownership of your photos and videos. You give us permission to store them, process them, and show them where you choose to share them.
- Videos you share are public to anyone with the link.
- You are responsible for having the right to photograph the job and for not including people's faces, number plates or private papers in a shared video.

## Data we may use
- We may use anonymised, combined information from jobs and the items collected (for example what kinds of waste are collected, in what quantities, and how often) for research and reporting. It will not identify you, your customers or an address.

## Records
- We keep each job's record for at least two years, even if you delete the photos and video, and we keep the tip receipt photo with it, because carriers are expected to keep their waste records.
